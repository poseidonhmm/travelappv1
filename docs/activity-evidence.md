# Activity evidence seed catalogue

Research checked on **22 October 2026**. Seven records cover the existing destination IDs `rotorua`, `lisbon` and `kyoto`. The other 285 destinations have no activity research in this initial catalogue. Missing data stays null or empty; no activities, durations or prices are generated from destination names.

Every activity and every numerical price starts **Ready for review**. Nothing in this seed is human approved. `location.verified`, `seasonality.verified`, consideration `verified` and `price.unitVerified` describe direct source support for a specific fact; they never mean that a human has approved the activity. The separate `verification.status` and `price.reviewStatus` control editorial approval. Recheck the source before booking; research does not guarantee future operation or availability.

## Confidence and review policy

These suggested age thresholds are Wanderly product policy, not facts supplied by the operators. Price confidence must be calculated from evidence, never from the plausibility of an AI answer:

- **High:** checked within 180 days; official attraction or operator evidence; clearly identified product, currency, pricing unit and inclusions; no unresolved conflict.
- **Medium:** credible indirect evidence or official evidence checked 181–365 days ago, with an identifiable product and unit. Use source-supported ranges only. Explain uncertainty such as the Te Puia currency confirmation.
- **Low:** evidence older than 365 days, incomplete unit/currency/inclusions, unresolved contradictory amounts or otherwise uncertain information. Flag for manual review and exclude from usable totals.
- Missing or invalid dates, future dates, absent URLs, negative or non-finite amounts, invalid currency, reversed ranges and missing unit support must not produce usable numerical estimates.
- A price can have High evidence confidence while still awaiting editorial approval. Show both states. No automated process may change Draft, Ready for review or Needs rechecking to Approved.
- Editing price evidence after approval should return that price to review. Editing a traveller's itinerary never alters catalogue verification.
- Track currencies and pricing units separately. Never sum different currencies or multiply group prices by traveller count without a declared group capacity.
- Unknown prices remain unknown, including potentially free activities. Zero is valid only when a source explicitly supports free entry for the exact covered eligibility group.
- Any displayed sum is an estimate of activity costs only. Show how many itinerary activities have usable evidence and how many have unknown or excluded prices. Never label it a holiday total.

Manual review is the final safeguard. Before publication, a human should open each linked source, confirm the exact product and currency, resolve the castle conflict, recheck accessibility and safety notes, and approve the activity and price independently. Expansion should follow the same sourced structure for the remaining destinations.
